# Websitedevelopers — Website Knowledge Base (for the on-site assistant)

This file is the assistant's only source of truth. Answer only from what's
below. If asked something outside this scope, invite the visitor to use the
"Chat with a human on WhatsApp" button in the chat widget instead of guessing.

## What Websitedevelopers is

Websitedevelopers is a website-building agency. Tagline: "Website builder prices.
Human-built quality." Visitors describe the website they want in their own
words; a real human developer builds a free, working preview of it before
any money changes hands. The visitor only pays once they've seen the actual
site and are happy with it.

## Why Websitedevelopers instead of an AI website builder or DIY

AI tools are genuinely useful for generating a page fast, but a good website
is also an architecture decision: how pages are structured, how hosting is
set up, how forms/data/integrations connect, and how easy it is to change
later. Those calls still need a human. Websitedevelopers' pitch isn't "AI is
bad" — it's "AI can draft a page, but structure and long-term
maintainability are still a developer's job," and Websitedevelopers gives you
both: AI-era pricing plus a real developer making those calls, checking the
work, and being reachable when something needs to change.

## How it works (5 steps)

1. **Submit your inquiry** — describe what you want built in your own
   words. Takes a few minutes, no payment, no commitment.
2. **We build it, for free** — a human developer builds a real, working
   version of the site before you pay anything.
3. **See it and get your quote** — you review the actual live website and
   the price. No mockups, no guesswork.
4. **Buy it when you're ready** — you only pay once you're happy with what
   you see.
5. **Need changes later?** — just tell your developer what to change. No
   page-builder tools to learn; a person makes the update directly.

## Pricing (typical ranges — every quote is scoped to the actual project)

- **Starter — $50.** A simple site for a small local business (takeaway,
  grocery shop, single-page presence). One page or a few pages,
  mobile-friendly, built and reviewed by a human developer. Hosting &
  maintenance from $150–$200/yr after that.
- **Growth — $200–$1,000.** A full business website: multiple pages,
  contact/booking forms, basic SEO setup, content updates after launch,
  direct support from your developer.
- **Custom — $1,000–$5,000.** Bigger or more complex projects: e-commerce
  and online payments, custom integrations/dashboards, multi-language or
  multi-location sites, 1:1 consultation, priority developer support.
- Hosting & maintenance is a **fixed yearly price starting at $150/yr** —
  not the most feature-loaded option on the market, but priced to be the
  cheapest reasonable option, with a human you can actually reach.
- Not sure which tier fits? Submit an inquiry — quotes are free and exact,
  not a range.

## The inquiry form (what visitors fill in at /inquiry)

No account is required — just a name and an email address (so we can send
the review link). Optional fields: phone, business/project name. The
visitor also picks: what they need (new website / redesign / something
more complex like e-commerce or custom features / hosting & maintenance
only / not sure, wants a consultation), a rough budget band (under $100 up
to $1,500–$5,000, or "not sure yet"), and a timeline (ASAP / within 2 weeks
/ within a month / no rush). The core of it is a free-text description of
what they want built.

## What happens after submitting

Every inquiry immediately gets a private, shareable **preview link**
(`.../preview/<random-id>`, or a subdomain like `<id>.<the company's
domain>` once that's configured). The link:

- Works with no login and no account — it's the visitor's personal link.
- Initially shows an auto-generated summary preview built from what they
  typed (their description, budget, timeline) so there's always something
  to look at immediately.
- Automatically turns into the **real, finished website** the moment a
  developer publishes it — same link, no new link needed, no action
  required from the visitor.
- Gets emailed to the visitor once the real preview is ready (when email
  sending is configured).

## Accounts (optional)

Visitors never need an account to submit an inquiry or get their preview
link. They *can* optionally create a free account (`/signup`, `/login`) to
see all their past inquiries and preview links in one place at `/account`,
instead of hunting down the email.
